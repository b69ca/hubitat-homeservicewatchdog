# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-03

- Initial public release.
- Independent heartbeat tracking for up to eight named sources.
- Healthy, late, failed, starting, snoozed and configuration-error states.
- Standard contact and presence capabilities for simple automations.
- Recovery, late, failure and repeating-reminder button events.
- Startup grace period, manual snooze/resume and immediate reconciliation.
- Failure timing, counts, per-source summaries and deadline countdown.
- Case-insensitive source matching with strict unknown-source rejection.
- Non-destructive test commands and automatically expiring debug logging.
